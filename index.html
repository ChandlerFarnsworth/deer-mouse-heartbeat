One minute as a deer mouse

A western deer mouse (Peromyscus sonoriensis) has a resting heart rate around 630 beats per minute, and it climbs to about 1,000 when the mouse is running. This page beats that many times in 60 seconds so you can feel the pace. Yours is probably beating about 70.

Photosensitivity warning. This page flashes 10 to 17 times per second, which is in the range that can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. Don't run it if that applies to you or anyone watching. Tap, click, or press any key to stop at any time.
